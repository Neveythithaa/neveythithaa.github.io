I am pursuing an M.Tech in VLSI Design at SRM Institute of Science and Technology. My primary interests are ASIC Physical Design, semiconductor implementation, RTL design, timing closure and low-power architectures.

I completed a Physical Design training programme at Takshila Institute of VLSI Technologies, gaining exposure to the RTL-to-GDSII flow using Synopsys IC Compiler. The training covered floorplanning, placement, clock tree synthesis, routing, static timing analysis, timing closure, TCL and Linux.

Alongside my coursework, I have implemented an 8-bit clock-gated processing unit using Verilog, OpenLane and the SKY130A PDK, and co-authored research on neuromorphic and quantum-inspired VLSI architectures.

Projects & implementation

A selection of academic and hands-on projects, with emphasis on implementation evidence and the tools used.

Low-Power Clock-Gated Processing Unit

An 8-bit processing unit supporting ADD, SUB, AND and OR operations. The clock-gated implementation uses the SKY130 integrated clock-gating cell and was taken through an OpenLane RTL-to-GDSII flow.

Featured project
25%Reported power reduction168 µW → 126 µW
2.32 nsCritical pathBoth reported variants
100 MHzTarget frequencyEvaluated flow target

Implementation highlights

  • Developed Verilog RTL and performed functional simulation with Icarus Verilog.
  • Integrated the SKY130A ICG cell sky130_fd_sc_hd__dlclkp_1.
  • Completed synthesis, floorplanning, placement, CTS, routing and timing analysis in the OpenLane flow.
  • Inspected the generated physical layout using KLayout and reviewed DRC/LVS results.
  • Compared ungated and clock-gated results across power, delay, area, PDP and EDP.

B.E. · Team project · 2024

Environmental Monitoring with LoRa Connectivity for Human Safety

Developed a team-based environmental monitoring prototype using Arduino, sensors and LoRa wireless communication. The project report describes DHT11 temperature/humidity sensing, MQ135 air-quality sensing and fire detection, with SX1278 LoRa transmitter/receiver circuits for remote data transfer. Solar and piezoelectric energy harvesting were also explored in the proposed system.
